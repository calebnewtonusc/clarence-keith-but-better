# Research

This site is a parody on purpose. The brief inverts the UX engine: every refusal ux-pick printed (no gradients, max weight 600 to 700, soft corners) is broken deliberately.

## Source: clarencekeith.com (scraped 2026-11-19)

- Title: "Clarence Keith, Founder, Artist, Builder"
- Hero text: "Build the World"
- Tagline: "Founder · Artist · Learner · Philosopher"
- Bio opens: "I've never been able to accept a box. Not because I'm restless, because the box has never made sense to me."
- Sections numbered 01 to 07: What I'm Building, How I Think, What I've Made, How I Work, Where I'm From, Who's In My Corner, What I'm Thinking About

Writing: em dashes, "not just X, a Y", "not only but also", choppy fragment stacks, rule of three, colon reveals, "Here's the thing", "In today's fast-paced world", delve, tapestry, testament, leverage, seamless, robust, game-changer, paradigm shift, "In conclusion", fake-profound kicker, leftover "Certainly!" and "Let me know if you'd like changes", [Insert X here] placeholders, lorem ipsum, "As an AI language model", knowledge-cutoff line, stale 2024 copyright.
